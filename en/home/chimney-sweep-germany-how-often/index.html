How often does the chimney sweep come in Germany?

Your chimney inspection notice (Feuerstättenbescheid) states when sweeping or measurements are due – this depends on your heating. In addition, a fire safety inspection (Feuerstättenschau) takes place twice in seven years.

Details

The Feuerstättenschau may take place no earlier than three years and should take place no later than five years after the previous one (§ 14 SchfHwG). The district chimney sweep checks operational and fire safety.

All other dates (sweeping, measuring, checking) are set in the Feuerstättenbescheid. Any chimney sweep business registered in the trade register may do this work.
