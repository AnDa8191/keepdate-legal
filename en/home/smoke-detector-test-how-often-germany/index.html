How often do smoke detectors need to be tested in Germany?

Smoke detectors should be tested at least once a year. The DIN 14676 standard provides for an annual inspection.

Details

Smoke detectors are mandatory in all 16 German states. Who has to do the yearly check – owner or occupant – is regulated by each state.

A smoke detector should be replaced after ten years at the latest, or earlier if the manufacturer says so.
