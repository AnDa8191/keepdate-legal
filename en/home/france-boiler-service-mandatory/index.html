How often must a boiler be serviced in France?

In France, an oil, gas, wood, coal or multi-fuel boiler of 4 to 400 kW must be serviced every calendar year. The heating engineer issues a service certificate within 15 days of the visit.

Details

For a new boiler, the first service must take place at the latest in the calendar year after installation. In a rented home, the tenant calls the heating engineer, unless the lease says the landlord does it.

The legal basis is Articles R224-41-4 to R224-41-9 of the French Environment Code and the order of 15 September 2009.

Tip: keep the certificate; tenants must keep it for at least 2 years.

Sources
