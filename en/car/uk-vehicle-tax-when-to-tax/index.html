Do you have to tax your vehicle in the UK even if it is exempt?

Yes: in the UK you must tax your vehicle even if it is exempt and you pay nothing. Only a vehicle registered as off the road (SORN) does not need to be taxed.

Details

To tax, you need the reference number from a recent DVLA vehicle tax reminder or ‘last chance’ warning letter, or from your log book (V5C) or its green new keeper slip.

If you have none of these, you need to apply for a new log book, and you can tax the vehicle at the same time.

Tip: enter the date your current tax runs out; the app reminds you two weeks before.
