How often does a car need the main inspection (HU) in Germany?

In Germany, a passenger car needs the main inspection (Hauptuntersuchung, HU) every 24 months. For a new car, the first HU is due after 36 months.

Details

The due date is shown on the inspection sticker on the rear number plate and in the vehicle registration document. What counts is the month: the HU must be done by the end of that month.

The legal basis is § 29 StVZO with Annex VIII. Rental cars and some special cases have different intervals.

Tip: book a few weeks ahead so there is time for repairs and a re-inspection if needed.

Sources

  • StVZO Anlage VIII (Hauptuntersuchung)
