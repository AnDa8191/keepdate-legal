When is an MOT due?

In Great Britain, a car needs its first MOT by the third anniversary of its registration. After that, an MOT lasts a year, so the test is due every year.

Details

You can get an MOT up to a month (minus a day) before it runs out and keep the same renewal date. If you test earlier, the new expiry date is counted from the day you pass.

Some vehicles must be tested when they are one year old. Northern Ireland has different rules and processes.

Tip: enter your MOT expiry date; the app reminds you a month ahead, when you can test without losing days.
