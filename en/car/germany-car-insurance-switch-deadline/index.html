When is the deadline to switch car insurance in Germany?

Most German car insurance contracts run until 31 December and can be cancelled with one month’s notice. The cancellation must therefore reach the insurer by 30 November at the latest.

Details

What counts is receipt by the insurer, not the sending date. If you cancel by post, allow a few days.

Not every contract follows the calendar year – check the contract year in your policy. After a premium increase under a tariff adjustment (§ 40 VVG) or after a claim, you can also cancel extraordinarily – the notice must then reach the insurer within one month.

Tip: add the deadline with some lead time so you have time to compare offers.

Sources

  • Kfz-Versicherung kündigen – Verbraucherzentrale Hamburg
  • § 40 VVG Kündigung bei Prämienerhöhung

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
