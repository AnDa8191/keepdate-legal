How often does a motorbike need the contrôle technique in France?

In France, motorbikes, scooters and quads (category L) need a contrôle technique every 3 years. For vehicles first registered from 2022, the first test is due within the 6 months before the 5th anniversary of first registration.

Details

For vehicles registered in 2020 and 2021, the first test must take place in 2026, no later than 4 months after the anniversary of first registration and no later than 31 December 2026. Some vehicles are exempt.

The deadline for the next test is shown on the sticker on the registration certificate (carte grise).

Tip: enter the date of the last test in the app to be reminded in time.

Sources
