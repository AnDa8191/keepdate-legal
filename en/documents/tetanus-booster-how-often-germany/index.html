How often do you need a tetanus booster (Germany, STIKO)?

Germany’s vaccination committee STIKO recommends that adults with complete basic immunisation get a tetanus and diphtheria booster (Td) every 10 years. The next due booster should once be given with whooping cough protection (Tdap).

Details

For deep or dirty wounds, a booster is recommended if the last dose was more than 5 years ago.

The date of your last dose is in your vaccination record. According to the RKI, STIKO plans to review the 10-year interval – if in doubt, ask your doctor.

This page does not replace medical advice.
