How often do you need to renew a Spanish driving licence?

In Spain, a category B driving licence is valid for 10 years as long as the holder is under 65. From 65, it is valid for 5 years at a time.

Details

To extend it, you must show that you still have the physical and mental fitness required for the licence. The application goes to the provincial traffic office (Jefatura Provincial de Tráfico).

The legal basis is Articles 12 and 13 of the Spanish General Drivers Regulation (Royal Decree 818/2009). If the holder has a condition that may get worse, the validity can be shorter.

Tip: enter the expiry date shown on the licence, and the app reminds you in time.

Sources

  • Real Decreto 818/2009, arts. 12–13 (Reglamento General de Conductores) – BOE

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
