How long is a French driving licence valid?

In France, the credit-card format driving licence is generally valid for 15 years. Renewal is an administrative procedure: you do not have to retake the category B test.

Details

The old pink 3-part paper licence generally stays valid until 19 January 2033. Validity can be shorter for medical reasons or for heavy-vehicle categories.

Renewal also lets you update your address and photo.

Tip: enter the expiry date shown on the licence; the app reminds you six months ahead.
