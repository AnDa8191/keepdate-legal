How long is a Spanish passport valid?

A Spanish passport is valid for 10 years if the holder is 30 or older when it is issued, and for 5 years if younger. For children under 5, validity is limited to 2 years.

Details

The validity cannot be extended: once it expires, you need a new passport.

The legal basis is Article 5 of Spanish Royal Decree 896/2003.

Tip: before a trip, check how much remaining validity your destination country requires.

Sources

  • Real Decreto 896/2003, art. 5 (pasaporte) – BOE

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
