How often does a towed caravan need the ITV inspection in Spain?

In Spain, a towed caravan of category O2 (750 kg to 3.5 t) is exempt from the ITV until it is 6 years old. After that it must be inspected every 2 years.

Details

As with cars, if the inspection takes place within the 30 calendar days before expiry, the new validity is counted from the expiry date.

The legal basis is Article 6 of Spanish Royal Decree 920/2017.

Tip: enter the day the caravan reaches 6 years since registration as the first date.

Sources

  • Real Decreto 920/2017, art. 6 (ITV) – BOE

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
