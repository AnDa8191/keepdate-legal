How often do motorhomes and caravans need the main inspection (HU) in Germany?

Motorhomes up to 3.5 t need the HU every 24 months, the first after 36 months. Motorhomes over 3.5 t up to 7.5 t need it every 24 months for the first 6 years and yearly after that.

Details

Caravans count as trailers: up to 750 kg gross weight (or without their own brakes) the first HU is due after 36 months, then every 24 months. Above 750 kg up to 3.5 t the interval is 24 months.

A 100 km/h approval does not change the HU interval. The legal basis is § 29 StVZO with Annex VIII.

Since June 2025 the gas system check is a separate obligation and not part of the HU.

Sources

  • StVZO Anlage VIII (Hauptuntersuchung)

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
