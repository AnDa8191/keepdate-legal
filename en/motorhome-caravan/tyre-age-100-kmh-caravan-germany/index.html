How old may caravan tyres be for the 100 km/h approval in Germany?

For 100 km/h, the trailer’s tyres must be less than 6 years old. What counts is the manufacturing date in the DOT code on the tyre sidewall.

Details

The tyres must also have at least speed index L. If they are older than 6 years, the 100 km/h approval remains, but the limit drops back to 80 km/h.

The basis is the 9th exemption ordinance to the German road traffic regulations (StVO). The rule only matters for combinations that use 100 km/h.

The DOT code ends with four digits: week and year of manufacture, for example 2321 for week 23 of 2021.

Sources

  • 9. Ausnahmeverordnung zur StVO – buzer.de
  • 100 km/h-Zulassung für Gespanne – TÜV Nord

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
