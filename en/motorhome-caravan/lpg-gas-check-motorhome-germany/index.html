Is the gas system check mandatory for motorhomes and caravans in Germany?

Yes. Since 19 June 2025 the LPG system check under § 60 StVZO is mandatory again and must be done every 24 months – separately from the main inspection (HU).

Details

It applies to keepers of registered vehicles with an LPG system that is not used for propulsion (for example heating, cooker, fridge). The check is due before first use, after relevant modifications and then every 24 months; the deadline ends at the close of the 24th month.

The test follows the DVGW worksheet G 607. For some years before June 2025 the check was not generally mandatory, so older articles are out of date.

Separately, gas hoses and pressure regulators should be replaced no later than 10 years after the printed manufacturing date.

Sources
