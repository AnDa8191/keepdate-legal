Frequently asked questions

What is Keepdate?

Keepdate is an app for iPhone and Android that reminds you of deadlines – for example your car inspection, passport renewal, smoke detector test or a contract’s notice period. Over 60 templates come with the interval and reminder times already filled in; you just enter the date.

Is Keepdate free?

5 deadlines are free. For more, Keepdate costs €2.99 per year or €9.99 once. If your subscription ends, all deadlines stay visible and keep reminding you – you just can’t add new ones.

Do I need an account?

No. Keepdate works without an account or sign-up, shows no ads and has no tracking. All your data stays on your device.

Which deadlines can Keepdate remind me of?

Anything with a date: car (inspection, tyre change, service, insurance), motorhome and caravan (gas check, damp test), home (smoke detectors, heating, chimney sweep), devices (warranty end, descaling), documents (passport, ID card, driving licence, visa) and contracts (notice periods, contract end). There are country-specific templates for the UK (such as the MOT), Germany, Italy, Spain and France.

How does Keepdate remind me?

With notifications on your phone, several times per deadline – for example 90, 30 and 7 days before and on the day itself. You choose the time. Overdue deadlines keep reminding you until you mark them as done; repeating deadlines then move to the next date automatically.

Which devices does Keepdate run on?

On iPhone via the App Store and on Android phones via Google Play. The app is available in English, German, Italian, Spanish and French.

How do I move my deadlines to a new phone?

In the settings, create a backup file and save it, for example to your cloud storage or by email. On the new phone, restore it with “Restore from backup”.
