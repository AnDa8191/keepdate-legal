How long is a pet’s rabies vaccination valid for travel in the EU?

The rabies vaccination is valid for as long as the vaccine manufacturer states – usually one to three years. The exact “valid until” date is in the EU pet passport.

Details

The animal must be microchipped before the vaccination and be at least 12 weeks old. After the first vaccination you must wait at least 21 days before travelling.

Important: if the booster is given after the old one has expired, it counts as a new first vaccination – with another 21-day wait.
