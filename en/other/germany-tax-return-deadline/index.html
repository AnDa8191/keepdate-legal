When is the deadline for the German income tax return?

If you are required to file and do it yourself, the German tax return is due by 31 July of the following year. For tax year 2026 the deadline ends on 2 August 2027, because 31 July 2027 is a Saturday.

Details

With a tax adviser or a wage tax assistance association, the deadline extends to the end of February of the year after next. If the deadline falls on a weekend or public holiday, it moves to the next working day.

The legal basis is § 149 AO (German Fiscal Code). The extended deadlines from the COVID period no longer apply.

The deadline only applies if filing is mandatory. Voluntary returns can be submitted later.

Sources

  • § 149 AO
  • Steuererklärung: Fristen – Stiftung Warentest

Checked on 2026-11-06. Rules can change – please check the source for your case.
